## 2. Core Observed Principles & NWW Derivations

| Reference | Core Observed Principles | Client Preference Alignment | Derived Principle Adopted by Next Web Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Linear** (`linear.app`) | Dense but calm visual hierarchy, crisp hairline borders (`#1E293B`), deep monochromatic slate canvas (`#0B0F19`), confident short copy, real UI artifacts over stock photos. | High information density, modern dark canvas, technical rigor. | **Spatial & Color Foundation:** 8pt grid system with 1px border dividers, Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter typography, and UI schematics modeling real code/API contracts instead of generic illustrations. |
| **Stripe** (`stripe.com`) | Structured modular service cards, clear typographic hierarchy, proof points positioned directly adjacent to claims, transparent feature breakdown. | Clear value propositions, modular capability breakdown, transparent pricing. | **Proof Adjacency Contract:** Every operational promise (24h quote, 100% white-label, 30-day warranty) substantiated with concrete mechanisms (written NDA, Indore jurisdiction, NWW CMS persistence) directly adjacent in the card. |
| **PostHog** (`posthog.com`) | Direct, engineering-first tone; transparent pricing tiers; zero corporate buzzwords; clear self-serve and high-velocity conversion paths. | Pragmatic, unpretentious developer tone; honest pricing and timeline framing. | **Voice & Tone Authority:** Grounded, peer-to-peer agency partner tone that directly addresses marketing agency founders without "overnight", "headache-free", or "game-changer" fluff. |
