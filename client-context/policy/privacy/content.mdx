# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Business Entity:** Next Web Works (Sole Proprietorship)  
**Registered City:** Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India  
**Official Contact:** contact@nextwebworks.in  

---

## 1. Overview & Commitment

Next Web Works ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website [https://nextwebworks.in](https://nextwebworks.in). We provide white-label software engineering, web application development, and technical consulting services to marketing agencies and corporate partners.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and safeguard information submitted through our website in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** where applicable.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We do not require account creation and do not track individual users across third-party websites.

### A. Information You Provide Voluntarily
When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, book a call via Cal.com, or subscribe to our engineering digest, we collect only the information necessary to fulfill your request:
- **Contact Details:** Your name, work email address, phone number (optional), and agency/company name.
- **Project Scope Information:** Team size, type of development requested (e.g., Next.js, WordPress, ERP, Mobile), estimated client request volume, and brief project notes.
- **Newsletter Subscription:** Work email address provided with explicit consent.

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data
When you navigate our website, basic non-personally identifiable diagnostic data may be collected:
- Standard server access logs (browser type, operating system, referring URL, timestamp).
- Aggregated website traffic metrics via Google Analytics 4 (`NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID`) with IP anonymization enabled.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business communication:
- To reply directly to your partnership inquiry or schedule discovery calls.
- To formulate project scopes and architectural quotations.
- To dispatch our white-label engineering digest (at most 2 emails per month), from which you can unsubscribe at any time with a single click.
- To detect, prevent, and mitigate security threats, spam, or malicious form abuse.

We **never** sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact details. We **never** share your agency or client information with advertising networks or third-party lead brokers.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following lawful grounds:
- **Consent:** When you explicitly submit a form or subscribe to our newsletter with consent.
- **Pre-Contractual Steps:** When responding to your request for quotes, technical scopes, or mutual NDA execution.
- **Legitimate Interests:** To ensure website security, prevent fraud, and maintain operational stability.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize reputable, privacy-compliant infrastructure providers to operate our business:
- **Form & Email Dispatch:** Resend (transactional email alerts dispatched to `alerts@nextwebworks.in` and `contact@nextwebworks.in`).
- **Discovery Call Scheduling:** Cal.com (embedded appointment booking).
- **Hosting & Edge Delivery:** Vercel Inc. and GitHub Pages (secure SSL-encrypted hosting).
- **Content & Lead Management:** Next Web Works internal CMS infrastructure.

All third-party service providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection standards.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Security

We retain inquiry submissions only as long as necessary to conduct ongoing business communication or satisfy legal, accounting, and tax compliance obligations. We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS), strict server access controls, and regular security audits to protect your data.

---

## 7. Your Data Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific rights regarding your personal information:
- **Right to Access & Portability:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your contact records.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Unsubscribe from our digest at any time via the link in the footer of each email.

To exercise any of these rights, email **contact@nextwebworks.in** with your request. We will respond within 30 business days.

---

## 8. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory modifications. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates the most recent revision. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
