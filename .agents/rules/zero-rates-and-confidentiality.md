# Zero Rates & Confidentiality Rules

## 1. Zero Rates Policy (Strict Invariant)
Under NO circumstances may any file in this repository contain:
- Hourly rates (e.g. "$X/hr", "from $X", "₹X/hr").
- Project minimums, retainer costs (e.g. "$X/mo"), or sprint prices.
- Discounts, payment split percentages (e.g. "50/50"), or rush fees.
- Rates in JSX copy, structured data (e.g. `priceRange`), metadata descriptions, form placeholders, alt text, or comments.
- **Approved Phrasing:** "Quotation in 24 hours", "Fixed-price quote", "Terms discussed on discovery call. No figures."

## 2. Zero Stock Photography Policy
- Strictly `stock_ceiling: 0`.
- Stock photography, generic corporate vectors, and unverified mockups are prohibited.
- All graphics must be in-house vector SVGs or authentic photographs (stock ceiling = 0).

## 3. White-Label & NDA Confidentiality
- Never invent client names, client logos, awards, or synthetic performance metrics (`FID < 50ms`, `90%`).
- Sample builds must carry the pill: `Illustrative sample · not a client project`.
- Commits appear under developer handles unless the agency requests authoring under their agency identity.
- Mutual NDA reassurance must never include conditional phrases like "on full payment".
